Blueberry Matcha Latte

Ingredients

  • 3g matcha powder
  • 50g warm water
  • 100g milk of choice
  • Blueberry syrup (see below)
  • Sweetener of choice: simple syrup, honey, agave (optional)
  • Ice cubes

Blueberry Syrup

  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • 1/2 cup sugar

Equipment

Bowl + bamboo whisk + sifter, or an electric whisk

Steps

  1. Make the syrup: Boil the water, then add the blueberries and sugar. Simmer until the blueberries soften, then mash and mix well. Strain to remove solids.
  2. Prep the matcha:
    • Bamboo whisk: Bloom the whisk by dipping it in warm water for a few seconds to soften the tines. Sift the matcha powder into a bowl, add the warm water, and whisk until frothy with no clumps remaining.
    • Electric whisk: Sift the matcha powder into a tall glass and add the warm water. Submerge the whisk into the liquid before turning it on. Whisk until frothy, about 30 seconds, or until the mixture is completely blended and smooth.
  3. Fill a glass with ice, pour in the blueberry syrup, then add the milk. Top with the whisked matcha and sweetener to taste.

Watermelon Orange Oolong Tea

Ingredients

  • Loose-leaf Oolong tea
  • Watermelon, cubed
  • Orange, peeled and chopped
  • Sweetener: honey or sugar (optional)
  • Cold or room temperature water

Steps

  1. In a glass container, add the tea leaves, watermelon, and orange. Mash slightly to release the juices.
  2. Add honey or sugar to taste.
  3. Pour in cold or room-temperature water, then stir or shake to combine.
  4. Refrigerate for 12 hours.
  5. Pour into a cup and enjoy.

Mango Smoothie

Ingredients

  • 2 cups, fresh or frozen, diced
  • 1/2 cup Milk of choice
  • 1/4 cup Water
  • Ice cubes (only if using fresh mangoes)

Steps

  1. Pour the milk and water into the blender first, then add the mangoes, and top with the ice.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour into a cup and enjoy.

Jasmine Milk Tea

Ingredients

  • Loose-leaf jasmine green tea (or tea bags)
  • 2 cups milk of choice
  • Sweetener of choice: simple syrup, honey, sugar (optional)
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. In a glass jar, add jasmine tea leaves and milk. Stir or shake well until the leaves are fully soaked.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for 12-24 hours.
  3. Remove the tea leaves using a mesh strainer.
  4. Stir in sweetener until dissolved, then pour over a glass filled with ice.

Blackberry Iced Tea

Ingredients

  • Blackberries
  • Water
  • Green tea (or tea of choice)
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices
  • Lime slices
  • Honey
  • Mint leaves
  • Lemon juice

Steps

  1. Blend the blackberries with water until smooth.
  2. Brew the green tea (or tea of choice) and add honey to taste.
  3. In a glass jar, combine the blackberry puree, ice cubes, lemon slices, and lime slices.
  4. Pour in cold water and the brewed tea into the jar.
  5. Top with mint leaves and a splash of lemon juice, mix well, then pour over ice.

Pistachio Iced Coffee

Ingredients

  • Pistachio syrup
  • Cold brew or espresso
  • Milk of choice
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Add milk, ice, and pistachio syrup into a cup.
  2. Pour in the cold brew or espresso.
  3. Stir and enjoy.

Ingredient Spotlight

Jasmine Tea

Jasmine tea is the oldest of all flavored teas, with the best coming from China's Fujian Province. It's made through a traditional scenting process: fresh tea leaves are layered with jasmine flowers overnight to absorb their fragrance. Jasmine's aroma is also used in aromatherapy, valued for its calming, mood-boosting effects.
