Fun Fact

Ceremonial Matcha

Comes from the youngest leaves of shade-grown tea plants, then are ground into fine powder. the quality of the powder can be found in the color. the higher grade matcha powder is usually bright green, while powdeer with a dull, yellowish tint usually means a lower grade or a powder that's gone stale. remember to keep it airtight in the fridge and use it within a few weeks of opening!
